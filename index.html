Legacy & Foundation

The Grand 9th Circle Story

The historical origins of the Grand 9th Circle trace back to 1912 in upstate New York, where American industrialist and literary patron Lord Sterling Alistair commissioned the design and construction of an expansive private estate. Drawing heavy architectural and geometric inspiration from Dante Alighieri's Inferno, Alistair designed the compound around nine distinct above-ground residential wings—which served as the structural foundation for the property's designation as the "9th Circle"—alongside nine subterranean vault levels.

The above-ground wings displayed late Gilded Age architectural opulence, incorporating vaulted arches, dark mahogany woodwork, imported obsidian marble, and heavy stained-glass features. Subterraneanly, nine reinforced steel vault levels were constructed beneath the main foundation to safeguard private reserves, gold bullion, and fine art collections. Upon its completion in 1918, the estate served as an exclusive retreat for North America's most prominent figures.

Following decades of closure after the Wall Street Crash of 1929, an international hospitality development consortium acquired the historical blueprints and artifacts in 2020. The consortium selected a high-altitude, forested parcel of land on the outskirts of Baguio City, Benguet, Philippines. Reconstructed between 2020 and 2024, the resort systematically replicated the 1912 gothic framework while integrating modern five-star infrastructure, seismic engineering, eco-friendly climate controls, and integrated Property Management Systems.

The Nine Wings Breakdown

Constructed according to Lord Alistair's 1912 masterwork plan, each wing serves a specialized luxury function.

Wing I Arrival Wing

Limbo

Main entrance featuring high-vaulted arches, check-in desks, concierge services, and guest reception lounge. (No guest rooms)

Wing II Honeymoon Wing

Lust

Dedicated strictly to private luxury suites designed for couples, featuring private valley balconies and stone fireplaces.

Wing III Imperial Dining Wing

Gluttony

Houses the estate's main dining halls, private banquet spaces, pastry kitchens, and subterranean wine cellars. (No guest rooms)

Wing IV Executive Wing

Greed

Exclusive accommodations equipped with integrated private room safes and direct access to subterranean vaults.

Wing V Wellness Wing

Wrath

Houses the hydro-thermal pools, fitness center, and Obsidian Sanctuary Spa treatment suites. (No guest rooms)

Wing VI Gallery Wing

Heresy

Features curated 19th-century artifacts, private library nooks, reading lounges, and luxury art suites.

Wing VII VIP Villa Wing

Violence

Ultra-exclusive, biometric-secured multi-room private villas for high-profile guests, diplomats, and VIPs.

Wing VIII Masquerade Wing

Fraud

Dedicated event spaces, high-society galas, Grand Masquerade Ballroom, private banquet halls, and host suites.

Wing IX Supreme Wing

Treachery

Top-tier wing exclusively housing the Lord Alistair Penthouse spanning the entire floor with 360-degree Baguio forest views.
